Ick

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Ick is a dating app for adults. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation in any form.

Adults only

You must be 18 or older to use Ick. The app asks for a date of birth before it opens and stays closed to anyone under 18, sign-up refuses anyone younger, and an account we believe belongs to someone under 18 is removed.

What is never allowed

  • Child sexual abuse material, real, drawn or generated.
  • Any content that sexualizes someone under 18.
  • Grooming, sexual advances toward, or attempts to meet a minor.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any other exploitation of a minor.

How we enforce this

  • Photos and profile text are screened automatically before they appear. Anything sexual involving someone who looks under 18 is refused and the account is flagged for a person to review.
  • Every profile and chat has a Report option, including a reason for someone who appears to be underage. Reporting also blocks that person.
  • We review reports within 24 hours. Accounts that break these standards are banned.
  • We report child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant national authority, and we cooperate with law enforcement, as the law requires.

How to report a concern

In the app, use the flag button on a profile in Discover, or the menu in a chat. You can also email us at the address below; you don't need an account to do that. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
