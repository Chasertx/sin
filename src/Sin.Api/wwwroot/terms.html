Community rules

The whole point is honesty about your own flaws. That's different from being cruel to someone else. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. You may not post or send:

Reporting and blocking

We review reports and act on them, usually within 24 hours, by removing content and banning accounts that break these rules.
