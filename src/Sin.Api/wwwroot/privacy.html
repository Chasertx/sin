Ick

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Ick is a dating app where people share their flaws up front. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your first name, your email address and an ID for your account from them, and use the name and email to fill in sign-up for you. We never see your Google or Apple password.
  • Profile: your display name, age, gender, who you're interested in, your headline, your confessions, and a photo if you add one. You can also add your height and your race or ethnicity; both are optional.
  • Approximate location: Ick is only available in the United States. The app asks your phone where you are and sends us a position rounded to about a mile. We use it to confirm you are in the US and to work out how far you are from other people. We do not receive or keep your exact location.
  • Filters: the age range, distance, height range and races or ethnicities you choose to see in Discover.
  • Activity: who you swiped on and how, your matches, and the messages you send to matches. We also keep a history of who you matched with, when, and when a match ended, which you can see on the History tab. When a match ends its messages are deleted; the history line stays.
  • Safety records: blocks and reports you make, and reports made about you.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse (such as repeated login attempts). Server logs are kept for a short time to fix problems.

We do not collect your contacts or advertising identifiers. Photos are re-processed when you upload them so hidden data such as GPS location is removed.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your profile to people who may be a match, show you people who fit your filters, deliver messages, and keep you signed in.
  • To keep Ick US-only: people who have not shared a location inside the United States cannot browse, be shown to others, or match.
  • To keep people safe: automatically screen photos and profile text for pornography and gore before they are published, review reports, remove content that breaks the rules, and ban accounts when needed.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • Other members see your display name, age, headline, confessions and photo, your height if you added it, and roughly how many miles away you are. They never see your location itself. Matches also see the messages you send them. Your email address is never shown to other members.
  • Race or ethnicity is not shown on your profile. Other members can choose to see only certain races or ethnicities, so someone who filters that way can tell that you fit their filter. If you would rather nobody could, leave it blank.
  • Service providers that host the app and its database and photo storage process data on our behalf. Photos, display names, headlines and confessions are sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to be screened for prohibited content before they are published. Private messages are not sent to it.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Possibly Icks → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, confessions, swipes, matches, messages and match history straight away. People you matched with keep only a line saying they once matched with someone who has since left, with no name. Reports you made about other people are kept so moderators can act on them.

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use Ick. The app asks for your date of birth the first time you open it and will not open for anyone under 18. That check happens on your phone: the date is not sent to us or kept once you pass. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we remove it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and confessions at any time, remove your height or your race or ethnicity, remove your photo by replacing it, block or report anyone, and delete your account. You can turn location off for Ick in your phone's settings, but Discover will not work without it. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
