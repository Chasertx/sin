Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Ick is a dating app where people share their flaws up front. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and an ID for your account from them. We never see your Google or Apple password.
  • Profile: your display name, age, gender, who you're interested in, your headline, your confessions, and a photo if you add one.
  • Activity: who you swiped on and how, your matches, and the messages you send to matches.
  • Safety records: blocks and reports you make, and reports made about you.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to limit abuse (such as repeated login attempts). Server logs are kept for a short time to fix problems.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers. Photos are re-processed when you upload them so hidden data such as GPS location is removed.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your profile to people who may be a match, deliver messages, and keep you signed in.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, remove content that breaks the rules, and ban accounts when needed.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see it

  • Other members see your display name, age, headline, confessions and photo. Matches also see the messages you send them. Your email address is never shown to other members.
  • Service providers that host the app and its database and photo storage process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to My Problems → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, photo, confessions, swipes, matches and messages straight away. Reports you made about other people are kept so moderators can act on them.

If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use Ick. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we remove it.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and confessions at any time, remove your photo by replacing it, block or report anyone, and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
